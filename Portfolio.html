Regulatory Compliance Audit

PBJ Daily Nurse Staffing: Q2 2024 (CMS Federal Dataset)

Engineered an auditable Python framework to validate 1.3M+ federal records from 14,564 U.S. nursing homes. Assessed facility-level non-compliance with federal staffing mandates and exposed reporting inconsistencies to mitigate regulatory risk.

The analysis covered 20 stakeholder-focused questions spanning national benchmarks, state-level comparisons, facility outlier detection, weekend staffing gaps, census-to-staffing correlations and full data quality audits, producing defensible executive dashboards for intervention.
