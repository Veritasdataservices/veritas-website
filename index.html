Founder's Perspective

“Too often, organizations rely on dashboards that look impressive but cannot withstand scrutiny. Decisions are made on numbers that have not been validated, metrics that are not clearly defined, and insights that can not be traced back to their source.” Veritas Data Services was founded to address that gap.

I built Veritas Data Services on the belief that analytics should do more than summarize data, it should diagnose problems, reveal root causes, and support decisions that leaders can stand behind. That requires rigor, not noise.

Our work is grounded in auditable, reproducible analysis, where assumptions are explicit, methods are transparent, and findings can be independently verified. Whether assessing operational performance, compliance risk, or workforce dynamics, the goal is the same: clarity that leads to action.

Veritas Data Services exists to help organizations move from uncertainty to informed intervention-using data not as decoration, but as evidence.

Sharon Mwandoe Founder & Lead Consultant, Veritas Data Services
