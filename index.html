Founder's Perspective

"Too often, organizations rely on dashboards that look impressive but cannot withstand scrutiny. Decisions are made on numbers that have not been validated, metrics that are not clearly defined, and insights that cannot be traced back to their source."

Veritas Data Services was founded to address that gap. I built it on the belief that analytics should do more than summarize data — it should diagnose problems, reveal root causes, and support decisions that leaders can stand behind. That requires rigor, not noise.

Our work is grounded in auditable, reproducible analysis, where assumptions are explicit, methods are transparent, and findings can be independently verified. Whether assessing operational performance, compliance risk, or workforce dynamics, the goal is the same: clarity that leads to action.

Veritas Data Services exists to help organizations move from uncertainty to informed intervention — using data not as decoration, but as evidence.

Sharon Mwandoe — Founder & Lead Consultant, Veritas Data Services

Our Services

How We Work

We build analytics systems that are auditable, reproducible, and decision-safe. Every dataset transformation and metric is traceable back to source data, validated through code, and designed for long-term regulatory governance.

  • Data Integrity Audit: Deep-layer cleaning and validation to ensure datasets are free from hidden noise before decisions are made.
  • Traceable Architecture: Every metric and calculation is documented and traceable back to raw source data.
  • Operational Governance: Long-term data workflows and inspection-ready documentation that preserve accuracy beyond delivery.
  • Evidence-Based Reporting: Dashboards grounded in verified calculations — built for intervention, not decoration.
