Android App Development

Mobile apps built with Flutter one codebase, native performance, faster delivery.

Mobile is often the primary way customers interact with a business in Kenya. We build Android applications using Flutter, Google's toolkit for building natively-compiled apps, allowing us to deliver polished, fast, reliable mobile experiences efficiently.

Why Flutter

Single CodebaseOne codebase can target Android and iOS, reducing long-term maintenance overhead if you later expand to iPhone users.
Native PerformanceFlutter compiles to native ARM code, so the app runs smoothly rather than feeling like a wrapped website.
Consistent DesignFull control over every pixel of the interface, so the app matches your brand exactly rather than looking like a generic template.
Faster DeliveryA mature, well-supported framework backed by Google means fewer roadblocks and quicker turnaround from concept to a working build.

Offline-First Design

Apps built to keep working reliably even with an unstable or absent internet connection, syncing data once connectivity returns.

Payment Integration

M-PESA and other mobile payment integrations built directly into the app where a business needs to collect payment natively.

Play Store Deployment

We handle the full path from development through to publishing on the Google Play Store, including store listing assets.
